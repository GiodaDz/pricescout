Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

PriceScout for Amazon is a Chrome extension that tracks price history and finds coupons on Amazon product pages. We've built it so that it requires no personal data from you whatsoever.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. PriceScout stores price history data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to any server.

What is stored locally on your device

PriceScout stores the following data only on your device:

— Amazon product ASINs (product identifiers) you have visited
— Historical price points for those products
— Timestamps of price observations

This data is used solely to power the price history chart and price drop notifications in the extension.

Affiliate Links

PriceScout includes Amazon affiliate links (tag: pricescout04-20). When you click "Buy on Amazon" or "Compare sellers" and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the extension free.

Third-Party Services

PriceScout does not integrate with any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar tools. The only external links are to Amazon and Keepa (for full price history), both opened explicitly by the user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties. There is nothing to share — all data stays on your device.

Permissions

PriceScout requests only the permissions it needs:

— Amazon domains — to read product prices and display the widget
— Storage — to save price history locally on your device
— Notifications — to alert you when a tracked price drops
— Alarms — to schedule background price checks

We do not request access to your browsing history, passwords, or any other sensitive data.

Price Drop Checks

To check for price drops, PriceScout's background service fetches Amazon product pages directly. This is done without opening any tabs and only for products you have previously viewed. No data from these fetches is stored or transmitted — it is only compared against your locally stored price history.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub page.
