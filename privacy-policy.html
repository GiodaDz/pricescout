Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

PricePal is a Chrome extension that helps you track Amazon prices and find coupons. We take your privacy seriously — in fact, we've built the extension so that it requires no personal data from you at all.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. PricePal stores price history data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to our servers.

What is stored locally

PricePal stores the following data only on your device:

— Amazon product ASINs (product identifiers) you have visited
— Historical price points for those products
— Timestamps of price observations

This data is used solely to power the price history chart shown in the extension widget.

Affiliate Links

PricePal includes Amazon affiliate links. When you click "View Best Price on Amazon" and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the extension free.

Third-Party Services

PricePal does not integrate with any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar tools.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties. There is nothing to share — all data stays on your device.

Permissions

PricePal requests only the permissions it needs: access to Amazon domains (to read product prices), and local storage (to save price history). We do not request access to your browsing history, passwords, or any other sensitive data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@pricepal.app.
